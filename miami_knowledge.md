## About
Miami Loves Green is a professional landscaping company in Miami, Florida. The team, led by AZ,
treats every outdoor space as a canvas and works to bring each customer's vision to life,
transforming yards into beautiful, healthy outdoor environments.

## Services
- **Landscape Design**: custom outdoor environments, meticulously planned and executed.
- **Hardscaping**: custom patios, walkways and water features built to last. Past work includes
  luxury pavers, gazebos, pergolas, turf paths and patios.
- **Maintenance**: comprehensive care that keeps the landscape vibrant (mulching, garden beds,
  ongoing upkeep).
- **Irrigation Systems**: efficient watering solutions for a healthy landscape.
- **Tree Care**: professional trimming and long-term tree health management.
- **Landscape Lighting**: elegant, efficient outdoor lighting.
